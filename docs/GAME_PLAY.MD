You are referring to **Liar’s Deck** from the game *Liar’s Bar*! It is a tense, high-stakes bluffing game where your poker face is quite literally a matter of life and death.

Here is exactly how a round plays out using that specific 20-card deck.

### The Setup & Deck

The game uses a distilled deck of exactly **20 cards**:

* **6 Aces (A)**
* **6 Kings (K)**
* **6 Queens (Q)**
* **2 Jokers**

At the start of a round, players are dealt a hand of **5 cards** each.

---

### Step-by-Step Gameplay

#### 1. Setting the "Table Card"

Before anyone plays, the game randomly selects a target rank for the round—either **Ace**, **King**, or **Queen** (e.g., *"King's Table"*). For the entirety of that round, **every card played must be declared as that specific card.**

#### 2. Making a Claim

On your turn, you must place **1 to 3 cards face-down** in the center pile and declare them as the Table Card.

* *Example:* If it is an Ace's Table, you lay down two cards face-down and say, *"Two Aces."*
* You can tell the absolute truth if you actually have them, or you can completely bluff.

#### 3. The Joker Wildcard

Jokers are your best friend. They act as **wildcards** and automatically count as the correct Table Card.

* If the table is "Queen's Table" and you play a Queen and a Joker, your play is **100% legal and truthful**.

#### 4. Calling "Liar!"

After you lay down your cards, the next player has to make a choice before playing their own hand: **Believe you** or **Challenge you**.

If they choose to challenge and call you a **LIAR**:

* **If you lied** (even one card doesn't match the Table Card or a Joker): You lose the challenge.
* **If you told the truth** (all cards match the Table Card or a Joker): The challenger loses.

---

### The Punishment: Russian Roulette

Losing a challenge doesn't just mean picking up the discard pile—it means pulling a trigger.

The loser of the challenge must pick up a revolver with a **1 in 6 chance** of going off.

* **If it clicks (Safe):** You survived! The round resets, all 20 cards are reshuffled and redealt, and a new Table Card is chosen.
* **If it fires (Bang):** You are eliminated from the game.

---

### How to Win

* **Empty Hand Safety:** If you successfully play all 5 of your cards without getting caught in a lie, you are safe for the rest of that round.
* **Last Man Standing:** The ultimate goal is simply to be the last player alive at the table after everyone else has eliminated themselves in Russian Roulette.
