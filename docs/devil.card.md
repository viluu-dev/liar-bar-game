 Liar's Bar: Devil Card Official RulesThe Devil Card is a high-risk, chaotic wildcard found exclusively in Devil / Hell Mode. It allows a player to trap their opponents into a mass penalty.1. Card AvailabilityThere is exactly one (1) Devil Card shuffled into the deck per round.It is dealt randomly to one player at the start of the game.2. Playing the CardStrictly Solo: You must play the Devil Card completely alone.Illegal Combos: You cannot pair it with any other card (e.g., you cannot play "Devil + King" to claim "Two Kings").Valid Match: It automatically acts as a legal match for whatever the current table card is (King, Queen, or Ace). If you play it, you are technically telling the truth.3. The Challenge OutcomesWhen you play the Devil Card, the outcome depends entirely on whether the next player challenges you by calling "LIAR":If Challenged ("LIAR" is called):The Devil Card is revealed.The challenge fails because the Devil card is a valid wildcard.Mass Penalty: Every single player at the table except you must immediately pull the trigger on their revolver in Russian Roulette.If NOT Challenged (Pass/Play proceeds):The card remains face-down.No effects are triggered, and its unique ability is spent.The game moves on to the next player's turn.
