# Liar's Bar: Devil Card Official Rules

The Devil Card is a high-risk, chaotic effect found exclusively in Devil / Hell Mode. Rather than being a separate card in the deck, it is an extra effect attached to one ordinary card already in play — letting its holder trap their opponents into a mass penalty.

## 1. Assignment

- There is exactly one (1) Devil Card per round. It is assigned when the game starts, and re-rolled from scratch every time a round resets (after a roulette pull triggers a reshuffle and redeal) — any unplayed assignment from the previous round is discarded, even if the same player would still qualify.
- Only an Ace, King, or Queen can be flagged — Jokers are never eligible (they're already a universal wildcard).
- The flagged card is chosen uniformly at random among all dealt eligible cards for the current round's hands, so a player holding more of the flagged rank has proportionally better odds of holding it.
- Only the holder knows their card carries the Devil effect.

## 2. Playing the Card

- **Automatic, not opt-in:** there is no separate action to "invoke" the Devil Card. Playing the flagged card completely alone automatically triggers its effect.
- **Strictly Solo:** combining it with any other card in the same play (e.g. "King + King") forfeits the effect — it's just an ordinary play, with no penalty or error.
- **Valid Match:** when invoked, it automatically acts as a legal match for whatever the current table card is.

## 3. The Challenge Outcomes

When the Devil effect is invoked, the outcome depends on whether the next player calls "LIAR":

- **If Challenged:** the challenge fails automatically. Mass Penalty — every player at the table except the holder must pull the trigger.
- **If Not Challenged:** play proceeds normally.

Either way, the Devil Card is spent the moment it's played — it won't trigger again until the next round reset rolls a new one.
